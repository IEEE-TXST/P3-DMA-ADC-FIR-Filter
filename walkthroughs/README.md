## Before you start

- Pause whenever the video says **Your turn**, and try it before watching the answer.
- Turn on subtitles in your video player to read along.
- These videos were made without a board, and the steps have not yet been tested on hardware. Screens marked **expected result** show what should happen.
- If your computer or board behaves differently from the video, tell your project leader.
- If a build says a driver file (`fsl_...h`) is missing, add that driver to your project or ask your leader.
